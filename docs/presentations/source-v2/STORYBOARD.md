## Audit of the previous deck (v1, archived in `docs/presentations/archive/en-v1-2026-10-21/`)
- Strong honesty, weak storytelling: 7 of 10 main slides use the same "light text block + one picture" pattern; 3 appendix
  slides read as the story's real content.
- No real fieldwork: the only photographs are bench shots of boards and probes plus one aerial. People appear only as
  an illustrated frog. The team, the boat crossing and the installed station (now available) were absent.
- One typeface at light weights everywhere → every slide has the same voice; nothing feels like a headline.
- Diagrams are generic (dots on a line, four stacked verbs). The progress bar is the only identity element.

## Art direction: "a living laboratory on a river island"
- **Photography leads.** Real images at full bleed or large crops; text sits in the image's own quiet areas (sky, water, shade).
  Grading: one restrained pass (slight contrast, no content change). Captions are a field log: date · place · what is shown ·
  what it is NOT evidence of.
- **The horizon line** is the identity device: a single hairline that crosses most slides at the same height (y = 900),
  carrying the field-log caption and slide number. Diagrams sit on it (07, 11).
- **Type (3 faces, all Vietnamese-capable):** Newsreader (editorial serif) for headlines and big figures; Be Vietnam Pro for
  text; IBM Plex Mono for field-log captions, units and labels. Scale: 120 / 80 / 44 / 32 / 24 px.
- **Colour, taken from the photographs:** deep canopy `#14231C`, river silt `#EDE6D8`, paper `#F7F3EA`, ink `#17201B`,
  silt brown `#A8865B` (secondary), HORIZON orange `#E8892B` (one accent per slide), sky `#9CC3DE` (rare, diagrams only).
- **Evidence glyphs (used in 07, 09, 10 and the appendix):** ● solid = observed in the field · ◐ half = built and software-tested ·
  ○ ring = designed / not yet proven. Always with a word, never colour or shape alone.
- **No** cards, icon packs, plate numbers or tracked-caps labels in the story slides. Motion: one fade per slide, plus
  step builds only on 07 and 11 where order is the content.
